# Envoy video storyboard

## What it is

A five-minute scene map for the current Envoy Remotion review cut and its final capture pass. Product recordings carry the story; motion graphics explain evidence and boundaries without depicting an action that did not happen.

## How it works

The review cut has synchronized English narration and captions. It uses a consistent monitor for web footage, a phone model for the SwiftUI simulator screen, and color motion around the devices. The scene order and current evidence are:

| Time      | Story beat                                                                            | Current review media and boundary                                                                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Open Envoy and show its guest entry point.                                            | Production guest composer, add menu, and model choice.                                                                        |
| 0:30–1:25 | Move through individual job postings, consultations, Nearby, and the native map.      | Production route recording shows 25 postings, four open consultations, and 78 Nearby results; the iPhone view is a simulator. |
| 1:25–2:15 | Ask the resident agent about a damaged bench and inspect the proposed report.         | Production Worker recording shows pending approval; Confirm stays disabled, no submission.                                    |
| 2:15–3:05 | Write a non-emergency report and inspect the Envoy-only review screen.                | Production recording stops before submission; no new receipt is shown.                                                        |
| 3:05–3:50 | Explain D1 outbox delivery and the dated Tiger aggregate observation.                 | Read-only 19:41 UTC query card, four delivered and four aggregate events from an earlier report.                              |
| 3:50–4:35 | Explain staff authorization and what still needs live acceptance.                     | Auth0–Worker–D1 diagram, not footage of a signed-in staff session.                                                            |
| 4:35–5:00 | Close on Envoy's current sources and report review, with the delivery boundary clear. | Production stills; no government delivery is implied.                                                                         |

Optional insertions replace time inside the five minutes only after live acceptance: an ElevenLabs voice follow-up with real transcript and reviewed submission; a physical-iPhone Presage reading that offers the calmer editor; a native SwiftUI application journey against the deployed API; or an Auth0 role denial paired with a permitted action. Never fill a missing segment with a mock provider response, synthetic success animation, or local development identity. Document fictional validation records in capture provenance and never present them as an actual resident, participating employer, or government action.

Selected web recordings were captured from the public Worker on September 26 local time. The captured site showed 25 individual official job postings, four open consultations, and 78 Nearby results. These counts describe the recording and may change after source refreshes. The iPhone simulator screen does not establish physical-device or production API acceptance. Auth0, ElevenLabs, and Presage flows remain conditional. The [Remotion project](video-production.md) records asset origins, hashes, and render commands. Before externally hosting the five-minute cut, the team still needs to approve the narration and keep the Envoy-only report delivery boundary clear.

## How to change it

Use `apps/video/evidence/production-browser-captures-2026-09-26.json` to locate and verify the active recordings. Update the spoken line, regenerate its audio and captions, and rerender if behavior changes. For a new accepted action, record its origin, build, timestamp, and redacted evidence in `capture-manifest.json`. Check legibility at normal playback size and rehearse the full five minutes after the final edit.

## Configuration

Capture the combined production Worker origin in English. Blur guest receipt tokens, Auth0 bearer tokens, provider keys, and personal résumé contents before exporting. Keep the capture log with capture date, URL, and whether the shot was live, cut from a genuine recording, or a diagram. The composition and local render commands live in [video-production.md](video-production.md); external video hosting is still pending.

## Dependencies

The core shots require the deployed web client, Worker, official source records, Workers AI, D1 feedback, and Tiger read-only access. Conditional shots require their respective live provider credentials and accepted device or role workflow.
